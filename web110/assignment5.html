In this session, I learned more about social media and how different websites and online services are used for communication, sharing information, and building communities. The activities helped me understand that social media is not only about posting pictures or messages. It also includes different ways that people connect, share content, organize information, and communicate with other users. I also learned about the social media honeycomb framework and how its elements describe how people interact online.

One activity helped me learn about different types of social media websites and their purposes. Some websites focus on sharing photos and videos, while others are used for professional networking, discussions, or short messages. I learned that each social media platform can have a different audience and purpose. This is useful for me because I am studying Graphic Art and Web Design. In the future, I can use different social media platforms to share my design projects and create a professional portfolio. Understanding each platform's purpose will help me choose the right place to present my work.

Another activity focused on the social media honeycomb framework. I learned that the framework describes different parts of social media interaction, such as identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. This helped me understand why people use social media in different ways. For example, some users mainly share information, while others use social media to communicate with friends or join online communities. I can apply this knowledge when designing websites because I need to consider how users will interact with the content and each other.

The third activity helped me learn about social media tools and online communities. I learned that websites can let users create communities, use public tags, share multimedia content, and communicate with others. One example is using tags to organize online information and make it easier to find. I can use this idea in my future web design projects by organizing content clearly and making websites easier for visitors to navigate. I also learned that users should be careful about what they share online because information posted on social media can become public and affect their online identity.
