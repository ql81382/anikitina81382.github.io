In this assignment, I learned more about how email works and practiced different email activities. I use email almost every day for college, work, and personal communication, so the activities helped me understand email better and showed me how to use it more safely and in a more organized way.

The first activity was learning the basics of email and practicing how to send, receive, and respond to messages. I practiced creating an email, adding a recipient, writing a subject, and sending the message. I also practiced replying to an email that I received. This activity was useful because I use email to communicate with my professors and at work. I learned that it is important to use a clear subject line and write the message professionally. I can use these skills when I communicate with my teachers, other students, and coworkers.

The second activity was learning about email client programs and how they work. I learned that programs such as Thunderbird can connect to an email account and send and receive messages. I also learned about email protocols such as SMTP, POP, and IMAP. I found IMAP especially useful because it lets email stay on the mail server and be accessed from different devices. I use email on my computer and phone, so this helped me understand why I can see the same messages on both devices.

The third activity was learning about email headers and email security. I learned that an email contains information such as the sender, recipient, date, and subject. I also learned that it is possible for an email address to be spoofed, so the displayed sender is not always enough to know if an email is real. This is important for me because I receive many emails for work and school. I will be more careful before clicking links or opening attachments from unknown senders.
