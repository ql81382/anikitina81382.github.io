During this assignment, I learned how to add different types of multimedia to a webpage using HTML. I learned the difference between raster and vector images and how to display them using the img tag. I also practiced adding audio and video players and embedding a YouTube video using an iframe. These activities helped me understand how multimedia makes websites more interesting and interactive. As a Graphic Arts and Web Design student, I can use these skills in my future projects to create professional and attractive websites. I can also use multimedia when designing websites for businesses to present their products and services in a more creative way.
